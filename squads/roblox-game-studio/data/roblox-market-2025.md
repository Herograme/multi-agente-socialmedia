## Daily Active Users (DAU)

| Quarter | DAU (Millions) | YoY Growth |
|---------|----------------|------------|
| Q1 2025 | 97.8M | +26% |
| Q2 2025 | 111.8M | +41% |
| Q3 2025 | 151.5M | +70% |

### Key Insights
- Roblox broke the **47.3 million concurrent users** record in 2025
- This beat Steam's all-time peak of 41.2 million CCU
- Average session time: **2.7 hours (162 minutes)** per daily active user
- DAU/MAU ratio reached **29.4%** in Q2 2025 (up from 20.92% in 2024)

## UGC (User-Generated Content) Trends

### Avatar Items & Commerce
- UGC sales surpassed **$330 million** in 2024-2025
- Avatar items and skins lead UGC revenue
- Creator Events contributed **$29 million** in direct payouts

### New Creator Incentives (Mid-2025)
1. **Daily Engagement Reward**: 5 Robux per active spender (10+ min in experiences)
2. **Audience Expansion Reward**: 35% revenue share on up to first $100 from new/returning users

### Revenue Share Changes
- Paid video games (launched Sept 2024): Up to **70% revenue share**
- Standard Game Pass: **70%** to developer
- DevEx conversion: Improved rates

## Key Trends to Watch

### 1. Older Demographics Growing
- 17-24 and 25+ segments expanding
- More sophisticated monetization acceptable
- Higher ARPU potential

### 2. APAC Expansion
- Largest and fastest-growing region
- Localization opportunities
- Different game preferences

### 3. Creator Economy Maturation
- Professional development studios emerging
- Higher quality bar expected
- More competition for visibility

### 4. Platform Records
- CCU records being broken regularly
- Viral potential unprecedented
- Single games can reach 15M+ concurrent

### 5. Mobile-First Reality
- 70%+ on mobile devices
- UI/UX must prioritize touch
- Performance optimization critical
